# ZED Box Mini or ZED Box 2 + TekaOS

Congratulations on being the owner of a brand-new [ZED Box Mini](https://www.stereolabs.com/series/zed-box) or [ZED Box 2](https://www.stereolabs.com/series/zed-box-2) with TekaOS!

![Illustration](https://storage.googleapis.com/docs-ouster-com/teka-docs/0.1.0/images/zed-box-with-tekaos.png)

Your [ZED Box Mini](https://www.stereolabs.com/series/zed-box) or [ZED Box 2](https://www.stereolabs.com/series/zed-box-2) comes pre-installed with TekaOS, Ouster’s operating system for deploying reliable edge apps scalably across your entire fleet of edge boxes.

This makes it a compact, cost-effective, and high-performance embedded system designed for lidar-based applications, featuring essential I/O, portability, and advanced connectivity.

With embedded [NVIDIA® Jetson™](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/autonomous-machines/embedded-systems/) modules, it serves as the intelligent core for fully autonomous AI systems, ensuring real-time processing and robust performance in demanding environments.

A key advantage of your [ZED Box Mini](https://www.stereolabs.com/series/zed-box) or [ZED Box 2](https://www.stereolabs.com/series/zed-box-2) with TekaOS installed is its ability to interface seamlessly with multiple Ouster lidar sensors over Gigabit ethernet streams, enabling high-speed, long-distance data transmission with minimal latency through the Ouster SDK’s [Streaming module](https://www.stereolabs.com/docs/development/zed-sdk/modules/camera/local-network-streaming).
