# Uninstall TekaOS from your ZED Box Mini or ZED Box 2

Want to remove TekaOS from your ZED Box and install Stereolabs' Ubuntu-based JetPack image instead? Flash the box using Stereolabs' script.

> **Warning:**
> Flashing erases the internal disk, including your apps, settings, and admin password. Copy anything you want to keep off the box first.

Stereolabs keeps the steps, scripts, and JetPack versions up to date here:

- [Flash your ZED Box Mini](https://docs.stereolabs.com/docs/products/embedded/zed-box-series/zed-box-mini/reset-update)
- [Flash your ZED Box 2](https://docs.stereolabs.com/docs/products/embedded/zed-box-series/zed-box-2/reset-update)
